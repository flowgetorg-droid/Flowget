# SparkCart Mall — Bangladesh Product & Market Analysis (2026)

Prepared: 20 August 2026

## What the current market signals

- Bangladesh online shopping is strongly fashion-led: a 2026 BSS report says fashion (clothing, footwear and sporting goods) accounts for the largest share of online purchases, while cosmetics, household essentials and electronics are also important. Cash on delivery remains dominant.
- TBS, citing BBS ICT Access and Use by Households and Individuals Survey 2024-25, reports that apparel-related purchases drive e-commerce; clothing is especially important outside major urban markets.
- DHL's Bangladesh e-commerce overview lists electronics (23.9%), hobby & leisure (20.4%), fashion (18.9%), furniture/homeware (12.1%) and care products (9.0%) among major online categories.
- Current Daraz category/search pages show sustained demand for mobile accessories, smart watches, power banks, home appliances, women's fashion, makeup/beauty and home products.
- Social commerce remains important, so products that can be demonstrated quickly in Facebook/Instagram video are prioritized in this catalog.

## SparkCart launch priority

1. Mobile Accessories — very high repeat/utility demand, low ticket size.
2. Women Fashion & Cosmetics — strong visual/social-commerce fit.
3. Smart Accessories — strong youth appeal, but keep quality control strict.
4. Home & Kitchen — practical problem-solving products are good for short-form video.
5. Personal Care & Grooming — compact, demonstrable, giftable.
6. Men Fashion & Lifestyle — easy cross-sell and gifting.
7. Smart Home & Lighting — visual products with strong content potential.
8. Computer & Gaming — focused audience and good bundles.
9. Car & Bike Accessories — niche but useful, especially rider-focused content.
10. Kids & Baby — repeat demand and giftability; product safety/age suitability should be checked before listing.

## Pricing strategy

For the first catalog, most products are intentionally positioned in roughly the ৳220–৳1,900 range to support COD-friendly impulse/utility purchases. Actual selling prices should be updated against your supplier cost, delivery charge, return rate and ad cost before launch.

## SEO structure

Every product includes: `name`, `category`, `details`, `specifications`, `sku`, `seo_title`, `meta_description`, `image`, `images`, `colors`, and `sizes`. The storefront can use these fields for product cards/detail pages and future product-specific SEO.

## Important

This is a market-informed starter catalog, not a guarantee that every item will sell. Test 5–10 SKUs per campaign, watch CTR → add-to-cart → confirmed-order rate, and restock winners.
